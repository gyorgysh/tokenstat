# Licensing and contributions

The [tokenstat Source-Available Licence, version 3](LICENSE) allows you to
share source code, contribute changes, and build and run your own copies.
pueev OÜ reserves distribution of builds, services offered to third parties,
and use of the tokenstat branding. This guide summarizes the licence; the
licence text controls.

## Permissions

| Activity | Without separate permission |
| --- | --- |
| Read, audit, copy or mirror source | Allowed; retain the licence and notices |
| Publish source forks, changes or build instructions | Allowed; identify modifications and the distributor |
| Submit code, documentation, translations or assets | Allowed under the contribution terms |
| Build, run or host your own version personally or internally at work | Allowed |
| Sell source copies | Allowed under the licence |
| Share source archives containing readable scripts | Allowed |
| Distribute binaries, installers, compiled libraries, APKs, IPAs or container images | Prohibited, even if renamed or modified |
| Publish builds through app stores, download pages, registries or beta channels | Prohibited |
| Deliver compiled client code, including WebAssembly, to browsers | Prohibited |
| Offer a hosted, managed, API or remote-access service to others | Prohibited, free or paid, even without delivering a build |
| Use tokenstat as product or service branding | Prohibited |
| Keep the repository name on a clearly unofficial source fork | Allowed |
| Describe your project as "based on tokenstat" | Allowed if accurate and without implying endorsement |

Activities marked prohibited require separate written permission from
pueev OÜ for rights it owns or can license. Third-party components retain
their own terms. Earlier grants and official-app agreements remain in effect
as described below.

You may share source and build instructions so recipients can compile their
own copies. Readable scripts remain Source Form even when an interpreter can
run them. Adding source to a binary archive does not exempt the binaries.

You need not publish private changes or submit them upstream. Personal and
internal use may rely on rented infrastructure or private CI acting on your
behalf. Keep build artifacts private. You may share reports that do not
contain the covered Software.

## Branding

Renaming or modifying a build does not grant distribution rights.
[TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md) allows accurate attribution, licence notices,
internal source identifiers and technical compatibility references. Clearly
unofficial source forks may retain their repository name. Private builds
need not be renamed.

## Contributions

You may develop and test your own fork without submitting it upstream. Open a
PR if you want us to consider a change. Maintainers may request changes, defer
or decline a proposal; submission guarantees neither review nor inclusion in
a future build. The contribution grant takes effect on submission with your
acceptance of the terms, whether or not the PR is merged.

Contributors retain any copyright they hold in their work. Section 6 grants
pueev OÜ the rights to modify, distribute and sublicense their contributions
as part of tokenstat and its derivatives under future licence versions or
other terms, including commercial and app-store terms, without further approval
or royalties. That grant survives licence changes. It also covers a
successor acquiring the project. A contribution grants no company equity,
rights in pre-existing code or trademarks, or contractual approval rights.
Statutory authorship and non-waivable rights still apply.

[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains acceptance and recordkeeping.
Maintainers must check the rights before merging or distributing a preview.
An authorized person must accept for agent submissions. Mechanical dependency
updates require a recorded maintainer review; dependencies keep their own
licences. The PR template records acceptance, but CI does not enforce it.

The existing grant permits pueev OÜ to relicense accepted contributions. New
contributor obligations require acceptance for future submissions under the
changed contribution terms.

A new repository licence does not obtain consent for old submissions. Check
existing grants and obtain any missing rights before distributing that work
under new terms. Contributors can grant only rights they hold or are
authorized to license.

## Official apps and earlier releases

Section 7 preserves existing end-user and store agreements, including
permitted re-downloads, updates, backups, and family or enterprise use.
This repository licence does not replace the app-store EULA. Apple's
standard EULA applies where no custom EULA has been supplied; a custom EULA
must meet [Apple's minimum terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/minterms/).

Earlier releases retain their original terms, including GPL grants for
GPL-covered material. Version 3 applies to copies published with it. Use
release-specific licence references when publishing builds so recipients
can identify the terms supplied with their copy.

The restrictions on build distribution mean this licence does not meet the
[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). The repository is
source-available. Third-party code, assets and notices keep their own terms;
the custom licence does not replace them.
